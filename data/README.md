# Data provenance

All included profiles, cases and policy mappings are synthetic engineering fixtures
created for this prototype. No doctor-validated training data was available.
Do not claim clinical accuracy from these labels. The emergency rules are an
illustrative shortlist, not an exhaustive medical protocol. Replace/validate this
policy with the released taxonomy and a clinical mentor before judging.

Keep organizer data under `data/private/` (ignored) until sharing rights are known.
The hidden CSV column mapping and batch follow-up handling are not yet confirmed.
